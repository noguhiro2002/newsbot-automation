# Code of Conduct

Newsbot Automation contributors must communicate respectfully and
professionally. Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private
information, and intentionally disruptive conduct are not accepted.

Project discussions should stay relevant to the software and should assume
good faith while allowing technical disagreement. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when conduct
places contributors or users at risk.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer. Do not post personal,
security-sensitive, or identifying information in a public issue.
